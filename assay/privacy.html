Assay — Privacy

Assay collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server.

Your photos

When you pick a photo, it is classified on your iPhone using Apple's on-device image classifier. The photo is not uploaded, not copied into the app's storage, and not shared with anyone. Assay keeps only the resulting estimate — a food name and three numbers — not the image.

Your food log

Your meals, body mass, goal and activity settings are stored in the app's own container on your device. They are not synced, not backed up to a Norea server (there is none), and are removed when you delete the app.

Network

Assay makes no network requests. This is not a promise about intent — it is a property of the binary. The build pipeline fails if URLSession, URLRequest, NWConnection or CFNetwork appears anywhere in the source. Writing a promise is not the same as keeping it, so the promise is measured.

Permissions

Camera and Photo Library are requested only so you can supply a meal photo. Both are optional: you can pick a food from the list by hand and the app works fully.

Health data

Assay does not connect to Apple Health. It cannot read or write anything there. Your daily burn is either a number you enter yourself or a table estimate — and the app tells you which one it is using, with its band.

Children

Assay is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
