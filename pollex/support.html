What is free?

The 3D wound map and what lies beneath, the flat map, the guided wound examination and call summary, the sensation map, every urgent page, the basic tests (FDS, FDP, two-point discrimination, perfusion), the first lesson, search and sources.

The 3D hand does not respond to touch.

It prepares the touch shapes in the first seconds after opening; the hint at the bottom reads "Touch the skin where the wound is" once it is ready. The flat map is always one tap away from the button at the top left.

I subscribed on another device.

Open a locked page, tap See plans, then Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found an error.

Tell us the page and the source; every statement names its source and corrections are checked against it.

Is Pollex medical advice?

No. It is a study and reference aid for clinicians and does not replace training, imaging, your hospital's protocol or the hand surgeon's assessment.
