Pollex: Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Pollex is a reference and study app for clinicians who see hand injuries. Nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What Pollex collects

Nothing. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Pollex does not collect, sell or share personal, health or patient data.

What stays on your device

Your lesson answers and review schedule are kept in your device's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Examinations and summaries

The guided wound examination and its call summary are not saved: they exist only on screen and disappear when you leave it. Pollex has no field for a patient's name or any other identifier. If you copy a summary, it stays on this device and is cleared from the clipboard after five minutes.

Purchases

Pollex Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your payment details.

Links

Pollex links to the publishers of the sources it cites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Pollex is for healthcare professionals and is not directed to children.
