Pollex: the 3D hand

Draft · 21 November 2026

The three-dimensional right hand and distal forearm in Pollex is adapted from open anatomy models. This page credits their authors and offers the adapted model under the same licence.

Credits

  • Z-Anatomy – The libre 3D atlas of anatomy. Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • BodyParts3D – The Database Center for Life Science. Licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

What was changed

  • The right hand and the distal forearm were cut out and the forearm sliced about 6 cm above the wrist.
  • Meshes were simplified and recoloured, and the skin was divided into flexor and extensor tendon zones and into nerve territories, as Pollex describes them with their sources.
  • A finger skeleton was added so that the examination tests can move the joints.
