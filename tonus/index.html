Every recovery app prints one number. That number hides how wide the uncertainty around it is, which baseline it was measured against, and whether the baseline itself has moved. Tonus shows all three.

Readiness comes back as a band, not a point. The baseline is on screen and watched for drift. When the same two signals — resting heart rate up, HRV down — could mean a hard training week or an infection beginning, Tonus separates the two and says which, and why. When it cannot separate two activities, it says so and names the one measurement that would have.

Everything is computed on your iPhone and Apple Watch. No account, no sync service, no analytics.
