Shockable

Emergency medicine: free resuscitation algorithms, a resuscitation timer, monitor drills, topics and bedside scores.

Coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. A free download with an optional Shockable Pro subscription. No account, no tracking, works offline. In English and Turkish.

Free

  • Algorithms: adult cardiac arrest, bradycardia and tachycardia with a pulse, and reading the first ECG in suspected ACS, step by step with two-minute cycle timers.
  • Resuscitation timer: elapsed time, CPR cycles, time since epinephrine, a 110/min metronome and an event log you can share. The log is never stored.
  • Emergency topics and scores: sepsis, anaphylaxis, stroke, trauma bleeding, DKA, head injury; GCS, qSOFA, CURB-65, PERC, Wells, Canadian CT Head Rule.
  • Core rhythm sprints and two code scenarios on a sweeping monitor.

Shockable Pro

  • Every code scenario: PEA, unstable bradycardia and tachycardia, torsades, SVT.
  • Rhythm sprints with the full rhythm set, weighted to what you miss.
  • The full emergency medicine question bank.

Shockable Pro is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription sold through the App Store. It can be cancelled at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Built on sources

The algorithms follow the 2025 American Heart Association adult advanced life support guideline and relevant ESC guidelines; topics and scores cite their guidelines and derivation studies by DOI or PubMed. Shockable shows no drug doses: follow your local protocol. Shockable is an education tool for health professionals and students. It is not a medical device and does not replace your training or clinical judgement.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
