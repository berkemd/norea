Shockable: Support

Restore Pro on a new device

Open Shockable, go to the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel or change a subscription

Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Report an error in the content

Every statement cites its source inside the app. If you think something is wrong or out of date, email the screen and the source; corrections are checked against the cited guideline.

Clinical use

Shockable is for education. It is not a medical device and must not be used to diagnose or treat patients.
