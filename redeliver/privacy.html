Privacy policy · last updated 18 November 2026

Privacy

RE:DELIVER has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. We, the developer, receive no information about you or about how you play.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: which rooms you have delivered, with how many couriers and steps, and how many hints you have.
  • Your settings: sound, music, motion and language.

None of this is synced or sent anywhere. In the app it is deleted when you delete the app. In the browser version it is kept in your browser's storage for this site and is removed when you clear that storage.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Apple, for the purchase. The full game is bought and restored through the App Store. The game learns only whether the full game has been bought. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.
  • Challenge codes. A code is created only when you choose to share one. It contains the room and the steps of your run, and nothing about you. It goes wherever you paste it.
  • The browser version is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host sees your IP address when the page is loaded. The page itself sends nothing.

The game makes no other network requests. Its music and sound effects are generated on the device.

Children

RE:DELIVER is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: brkekahraman@icloud.com.
