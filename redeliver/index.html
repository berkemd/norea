RE:DELIVER •

About & support · updated 18 November 2026

One short shift. Every loop, your earlier self does it all again.

RE:DELIVER is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. You have a parcel to deliver and not enough time. So you loop again, and the you from a moment ago is still there, repeating exactly what you did: holding the door you could not hold, standing on the plate you had to leave, passing the parcel through the counter to where you are waiting now.

Play the first two chapters in your browser

The browser version has the first 19 rooms. The app for iPhone and iPad has all 61, in six chapters.

What is in it

  • 61 rooms in six chapters, each chapter about one new idea: doors and plates, counters and weights, switches that let go by themselves, parcels with a right address, and rooms that need three of you.
  • Checked by an exact solver. Every room can be delivered, and none can be done with fewer couriers than it says.
  • Step by step. Nothing moves until you do. Step back one move, or take a whole loop back.
  • A limited number of hints that show the next step. Clean deliveries earn more.
  • English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español and 日本語. Plays offline. No account.

Support

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, your device and the room you were in.

How do I play? Swipe across the room or use the pad to walk. The round button picks up, puts down and presses. When your steps run out, or whenever you like, tap Loop again: what you just did is kept and replayed by an earlier you, and you start over beside it.

I am stuck. Hint shows the next step and costs one hint. Step back undoes one move; Take a copy back removes the last loop you kept.

I bought the full game on another device. Open Settings in the game, or the purchase sheet, and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account.

There is no sound. The game follows the ring/silent switch and the volume of your device. Music and effects can be turned on and off separately in Settings.

Where is my progress kept? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
