Routes that know the sun and the weather

Fairlead is an app for iPhone and iPad. Search for a place and it draws the way there in the colours of the trip: amber where the low sun will be in your eyes, blue where rain or snow is forecast, violet where it will be dark, for the hour you leave. Move the departure time and the route recolours. Then Fairlead guides you there with spoken, turn-by-turn directions.

Routes, search and directions come from Apple Maps. The forecast comes from MET Norway. The sun's position is worked out on your device.

Support

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, your device and the steps that lead to the problem.

How is "sun in your eyes" decided? For each stretch of the route Fairlead takes the direction you will be travelling and the sun's position at the minute you will be there. The stretch counts when the sun is above the horizon but low (18° or less at the standard setting), within 30° of straight ahead, the forecast cloud is under 70 % and it is not raining.

What it does not know. Hills, buildings and trees that shade the road; tunnels; the exact minute you reach each point (the travel time is spread evenly along the route, and traffic changes it). Treat the colours as a well-founded estimate.

The voice is silent. Check the speaker button on the guidance screen and the ring/silent volume. Spoken directions use the language of your device; the sentences Fairlead adds itself are written out in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch.

Guidance stopped following me. Fairlead needs location access "While Using the App". During guidance iOS shows the location indicator and Fairlead keeps following with the screen off; it stops when you end guidance or arrive.

Subscriptions. Manage or cancel Fairlead Pro in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. To bring a purchase to another device, open Fairlead's settings and choose Restore purchases.
