Privacy policy · last updated 12 November 2026

Privacy

Fairlead has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. We, the developer, receive no information about you, where you are or where you go.

What stays on your device

  • Saved places and recent searches.
  • Usual trips. When you start guidance, Fairlead notes the start, the destination and the time, so that after three similar trips it can suggest the next one. You can switch this off ("Learn my usual trips") and erase the history in Fairlead's settings.
  • Your settings, including how much sun, rain and darkness bother you.

None of this is synced or sent anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the app.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Apple Maps. What you type in the search field, and the start and end of a route (including your position when you start from where you are, and again if Fairlead has to find a new route during guidance), are sent to Apple to return places and directions. Apple's handling of Maps data is described in Apple Maps & Privacy.
  • MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). To know the cloud and rain on a route, your device asks MET Norway for the forecast at up to six points along it. The coordinates are rounded to two decimals, about one kilometre. As with any web request, MET Norway sees your IP address and that the request came from Fairlead. See MET Norway's privacy statement.
  • Apple, for purchases. Subscriptions are bought and managed through the App Store. Fairlead learns only whether Fairlead Pro is active.

Fairlead adds nothing to these requests that identifies you: no account, no advertising identifier, no tracking code.

Location

Fairlead asks for your location "While Using the App". It is used to start routes from where you are and, during guidance, to follow you along the route, work out the next instruction and notice when you have left the route. During guidance this continues with the screen off or the app in the background, and iOS shows its location indicator while it does. It stops when you end guidance or arrive. Your position is processed on your device; it is not recorded as a track and is not sent to us.

Notifications

A leave reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver it.

Children

Fairlead is a general-audience navigation app and is not directed at children.
