Ogee Support

Answers to common questions. For anything else, write to brkekahraman@icloud.com.

What is free?

All emergency pathways, the Safety first lessons, the face map and every region page, search and sources, the note on why toxin units are not interchangeable, and the lips cross-section. These stay free whether or not you subscribe.

What does Ogee Pro add?

The remaining lessons and their questions, the other cross-sections, and the label tools (dilutions, labelled doses, side-by-side comparison, filler families).

I subscribed on another iPhone or iPad. How do I unlock Pro here?

Open any locked page, tap See plans, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

In Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Ogee has no account, so progress lives only on the device where you study. Your subscription moves with your Apple Account.

I found an error in the content.

Please tell us which page and which source. Every statement names its source, and corrections are checked against it.

Is Ogee medical advice?

No. Ogee is a study and reference aid for licensed clinicians. It recommends no dose and does not replace the product's prescribing information, training or clinical judgement. In an emergency follow your clinic's protocol and call emergency services.
