Ogee

Filler and toxin anatomy, emergency pathways and sourced lessons, for clinicians who inject.

Coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. A free download with an optional Ogee Pro subscription. No account, no tracking, works offline.

Free, always

  • Emergency pathways for vascular occlusion after hyaluronic acid filler, vision loss after filler, anaphylaxis, eyelid ptosis after toxin and late nodules. Tick each step as you go, keep a timed log you can copy, and set a timer where the source protocol gives an interval.
  • Safety first: five lessons on recognising and managing complications.
  • A face map of twelve regions, with risk, muscles, vessels and nerves as layers you can switch.
  • Search across everything, saved pages, and every source listed in full.

Ogee Pro

  • Layers, step by step: animated cross-sections of the lips, nasal dorsum, nasolabial fold, temple and glabella that show tissue planes, vessels and the zones to keep out of.
  • All 17 lessons, 68 questions and spaced review.
  • Label tools: the dilutions each U.S. label lists, labelled doses product by product, six labels side by side, and the families of filler.

Ogee Pro is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription sold through the App Store. It can be cancelled at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Built on sources

Every clinical statement in Ogee is written from a cited source: peer-reviewed papers, official prescribing information and a plastic surgery textbook. Ogee recommends no dose. It does not replace the product label, your training or your clinical judgement, and it is not for patients.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising. Your progress, saved pages and emergency logs stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
