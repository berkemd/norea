Ogee: Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Ogee is a reference and study app for clinicians who inject dermal fillers and botulinum toxin. It is built so that nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What Ogee collects

Nothing. Ogee has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data, health data or patient data.

What stays on your device

To work, Ogee keeps a few things in your device's own storage: your study progress and review schedule, the pages you have saved, your recent searches, the running timer and the timed log of an emergency pathway. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The emergency log never holds patient details.

Spotlight and shortcuts

Ogee adds the titles of its own pathways, lessons, regions and drawings to your device's Spotlight index, so that searching your device can find them, and offers shortcuts that open an emergency pathway. The index and the shortcuts live on your device and hold Ogee's content only, never your progress.

Notifications

If you start a timer in an emergency pathway, Ogee asks permission to show a local notification when the timer ends. The notification is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases and ratings

Ogee Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your payment details. Ogee may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt; any rating you give goes to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to both.

Links

Ogee links to the published sources it cites (for example PubMed, publishers and DailyMed). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Ogee is for healthcare professionals and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
