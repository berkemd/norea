Isoline: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Isoline is a learning app for health professionals and students. It is built so that nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What Isoline collects

Nothing. Isoline has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data, health data or patient data.

What stays on your device

To work, Isoline keeps your case progress, review schedule, streak and question-bank statistics in your device's own storage. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Isoline Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Links

Isoline links to the published sources it cites (for example doi.org and PubMed). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Isoline is for health professionals and students and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
