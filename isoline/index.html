Isoline

Cardiology and ECG, case by case: new 12-lead tracings every time, a sourced question bank and risk scores.

Coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. A free download with an optional Isoline Pro subscription. No account, no tracking, works offline. In English and Turkish.

Free

  • Core ECG cases with full 12-lead tracings you can zoom and measure with calipers, and a new tracing every time you return.
  • The ECG atlas: recognition criteria and pitfalls for every finding, with an example tracing and its sources.
  • Cardiology topics from acute coronary syndromes to heart failure, AF, valves, lipids, pulmonary embolism and syncope.
  • Risk scores: CHA₂DS₂-VA, HAS-BLED, HEART, Wells, sPESI.

Isoline Pro

  • Every intermediate and advanced case, from STEMI equivalents to Brugada, long QT, WPW and hyperkalaemia.
  • The full cardiology question bank, each answer explained with its guideline.
  • Spaced review across the whole library.

Isoline Pro is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription sold through the App Store. It can be cancelled at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Built on sources

Every criterion, explanation and score in Isoline cites a published guideline or consensus statement (ESC, AHA/ACC/HRS and others), linked by DOI or PubMed. Tracings are simulated by a model of the heart's electrical activity; they are not patient recordings. Isoline is an education tool for health professionals and students. It is not a medical device and does not replace your training or clinical judgement.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
