Bisque — Support

Bisque is a toy: a white figure your child paints, which then wakes up and lives in a small place that is different every day.

Getting started

Open it and hand it over. There is nothing to set up, no account to make, no tutorial to sit through and nothing to read. A child finds the brushes by touching them.

Questions

My child can’t read yet. Is that a problem?

No — the app contains no reading at all. Everything is pictures and sound, and the creature speaks in sounds rather than words. The only text anywhere is in the parents’ section, behind a parental gate.

Where are the new outfits and characters?

There is nothing to download and nothing to buy. Each day the app generates a new visiting creature, a new sky, new brushes and a new little place, from the date and from what your child has already painted. It does not run out, and no two children get the same day.

Why so few clothes?

Because each one actually moves. A cape swings when the creature turns; a skirt settles. Five pieces that behave like cloth hold a child’s attention far longer than fifty stickers that don’t — and every piece can be painted, so the number of outfits is not really five.

Can my child make something ugly or scary?

Your child can paint anything they like — there is no wrong answer and nothing is rejected. What is constrained is what the app generates: every creature it produces is measured against written rules for proportion and colour, and across 200 000 generated creatures none broke one.

Does painting affect anything, or is it just decoration?

It decides the creature’s temperament. Warm colours give a livelier one, cool colours a calmer one, dark and dull colours a sleepier one. The rule is deliberately simple so that a child can discover it.

Is there a time limit or a reward?

No. No timers, no energy, no streaks, no stars, no levels. A toy is played with when its owner wants to play with it, and painting has no score.

Are my child’s paintings sent anywhere?

No. The app makes no network request of any kind, so there is no path for anything to leave the device. See Privacy.

Can I move the paintings to another device?

Only through your own device backup. There is no sync, because sync needs a server and a server needs your child’s data.

Is there an Apple Watch version?

No, and that is a decision rather than an omission: a painting surface does not fit on a wrist.

Something is wrong.

Tell us what happened and what the app did instead: hello@norea.studio. There is no crash reporting, so we only learn about problems if someone writes.
