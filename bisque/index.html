Bisque

Turn a little ceramic creature into your own colourful companion. Paint directly on its 3D surface, dress it up, and watch its character respond to your colours.

A small studio, ready to play

Choose from 14 colours and four tools: crayon, watercolour, glaze and glitter. Drag across the figure to paint. Drag on the background to turn it and reach another side. Add hats, scarves and other outfits with a tap.

Keep what you create

Your current artwork, clothes and selected tools are saved on this device. Return to the same creation when you reopen Bisque. Undo recent edits, or hold Start over to clear the paint and clothing; a short tap shows a hint without erasing.

Colour becomes character

A simple on-device rule turns the colours used into a playful mood: warmer colours encourage a livelier companion, cooler colours a quieter one. Soft movement and wordless sounds bring the figure to life. This is imaginative play, not an assessment of your child.

A quiet place to make things

The painting area uses pictures instead of written instructions. No ads, accounts, chat, in-app purchases or subscriptions. No scores, streaks or time limits. Painting works offline.

An optional watch companion

On an Apple Watch paired with your iPhone, see a simple animated companion inspired by your creature and its mood. The painted surface and clothes stay on the iPhone; the Watch is a companion, not a painting screen. Open Bisque on the paired iPhone to send an update. Delivery depends on Apple’s device connection.

For grown-ups

Artwork stays on the device, with no Bisque account or cross-device artwork sync. Bisque contains no ads or analytics and requests no camera, microphone or location access. Only creature identity and mood data pass to the paired Watch. Privacy and support links are behind an adult maths challenge and open in your browser.

For open-ended creative play on iPhone and iPad, with an optional Apple Watch companion.
