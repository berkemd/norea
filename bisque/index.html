A few things for grown-ups

What does the purchase include?

All 14 colours, four painting tools and six outfits are included. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Check the App Store for the current price in your region.

Where does the artwork go?

The current creation is saved on this device. There is no account, online gallery or cross-device artwork sync. Starting over replaces the current creation; it does not create a collection.

Do we need an Apple Watch?

No. Paint and dress your creature on iPhone or iPad. An optional Watch app shows a simple companion based on the creature's identity and mood from a paired iPhone. Paint and clothing do not transfer to the Watch. Open Bisque on the paired iPhone to send an update; delivery depends on the device connection.

What about privacy and external links?

Bisque has no ads or analytics and requests no camera, microphone or location access. The app's support and privacy links sit behind an adult maths challenge. This website is for grown-ups choosing the app.
