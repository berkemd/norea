Vird

A calm place for the things you want to remember.

You already write things down. Vird is what happens next: it turns a note you have already written into a small number of question-and-answer cards, and then shows them back to you at widening intervals — a day, three days, a week, a month — so the ones you keep getting right stop asking for your time and the ones you keep missing come back sooner.

Vird does not want your notes. Point it at the folder your notes already live in. It reads that folder. It never writes to it, never moves anything, and never uploads it.

What it actually does

Cards from your own wordsVird reads a note and proposes cards from what is in it — explicit questions you already wrote, emphasised terms, paragraph pairs. On device. No network involved.
Spacing that adaptsAn SM-2-family schedule. Cards you recall easily drift further apart; cards you fumble come back tomorrow.
Your library, plainlyCards are yours. Import from CSV or an Anki-format deck; export the same way. Nothing is locked in.
Optional AI, on your keyIf you turn it on and supply your own provider key, Vird can suggest sharper cards. Only a note's title and a short excerpt are sent — never the body.

Free and Plus

FreeThe full review engine, a capped library, import and export. Not a trial — it does not expire.
Vird PlusRemoves the cap, enables the optional AI suggestions, and mirrors your library to a folder you choose.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. There is no Vird account, because there is nowhere to have one.
