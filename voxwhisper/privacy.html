English

VoxWhisper collects no data. None — we run no server and have no account system. Your text notes do sync, through your own iCloud, which we cannot read. Audio memos never go to iCloud or to us — with the iPhone app they are copied directly from your watch to your own iPhone.

Two kinds of capture, treated differently

VoxWhisper does two things, and it is important that you know they are not handled the same way.

1. Dictated text notes — these sync through your iCloud

Text notes are saved on the watch and also mirrored into your own iCloud key-value store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore), so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That means note text does leave the watch: it goes to Apple's iCloud, under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy terms, and nowhere else. We have no access to it. There is no server of ours in the chain, no key we hold and no way for us to read, list or recover your notes. If you sign out of iCloud, notes stay on the watch and simply stop syncing. Deleting a note also publishes a deletion marker, so it does not come back from another device.

2. Audio voice memos — never to iCloud, never to us

Voice recordings are written as files into the app's own folder on the watch. They are never uploaded to iCloud, never sent to us, never shared and never transcribed on any server. If you also install the VoxWhisper iPhone app (version 1.8 or later), each memo is copied directly from your watch to your paired iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity link, so you can listen to it on the phone. That copy goes device to device; no server of ours or anyone else's is involved. Deleting a memo on the iPhone removes only the iPhone copy. Without the iPhone app, memos stay on the watch until you delete them or delete the app.

Turning a memo into text — on your iPhone (version 1.9 or later)

From version 1.9, the iPhone app can turn a voice memo into text. This uses Apple's Speech framework (SpeechAnalyzer) running on your iPhone: iOS downloads the recognition model and the audio is processed on the device. Neither the audio nor the text is sent to us or to any server of ours. The text is saved next to the memo in the app's own folder on your iPhone and is deleted together with the memo. It becomes part of your synced notes only if you tap Save as note. Nothing is transcribed until you tap Transcribe for the first time; that is also when iOS asks for Speech Recognition permission. Requires iOS 26 or later. From version 2.0, a memo that arrives after you have allowed Speech Recognition is transcribed without a further tap. Transcripts are part of VoxWhisper Pro; the first three recordings you make on the iPhone include them for free.

Recording on iPhone (version 2.0 or later)

From version 2.0 the iPhone app can record as well. A recording made on the iPhone is a file in the app's own folder on that iPhone. It is not uploaded to iCloud and not sent to us. It stays until you delete it or delete the app.

Summaries and action items — on your iPhone (version 2.0 or later)

From a transcript, the app makes a title, a short summary and a list of things to do. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, the wording is written by Apple's language model through the Foundation Models framework, running on your iPhone. On other iPhones, and in languages that model does not support, the app picks the speaker's own sentences with fixed rules, also on the iPhone. In both cases neither the transcript nor the result is sent to us or to any server of ours. Summaries are saved in the app's own folder and are removed when the memo is deleted. The same rules suggest action items for a text note when you open it.

Reminders (version 2.0 or later)

An action item becomes a reminder only when you tap Add to Reminders. The app then writes that one reminder (its title, the date you confirmed and a line saying it came from VoxWhisper) to your default Reminders list through Apple's EventKit, and removes that same reminder if you tap Undo. iOS offers only full access for Reminders, so the system prompt says the app can access your reminders; VoxWhisper does not read, copy, change or send anywhere any other reminder. Whether your Reminders sync, for example through iCloud, depends on your own account settings, not on us.

Dictation

Speech-to-text is performed by watchOS itself, through the system text-input controller. The watch app contains no speech-recognition code and never receives or stores the audio from dictation. How that system service handles your speech is described in Apple's privacy policy.

What VoxWhisper asks for

  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription), only when you record an audio memo. Dictated text notes do not need the app to have microphone access — the system controller handles that.
  • Speech Recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription), iPhone app 1.9 or later, only when you tap Transcribe on a memo.
  • Reminders (NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription), iPhone app 2.0 or later, only when you first tap Add to Reminders. You can turn it off at any time in Settings; everything else keeps working.
  • Nothing else. No location, no HealthKit, no motion, no contacts, no camera.

What the watch face is told

The complication runs in a separate process and is drawn on an always-on display that anyone standing next to you can read. It is therefore given counts only — how many notes and memos you have, how much space the memos use, and when the last capture was. Never the text.

Network use

VoxWhisper contains no HTTP client, no sockets, no analytics and no third-party SDK. The single mechanism that moves anything off the watch is the iCloud key-value sync of text notes described above, and it is Apple's, not ours. Audio memos copied to your iPhone travel over Apple's direct watch-to-iPhone link, which is also Apple's and never touches the internet through us.

Legal basis for anything that happens

  • Whatever VoxWhisper keeps on your watch — we are not processing it, because it never reaches us. It sits in your device, under your control, and is gone when you delete the app. There is no legal basis to state for us, because there is no processing by us.
  • Microphone permission — granted by you in the system prompt, revocable at any time in Settings; consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / KVKK Art. 5(1). Recordings stay on your watch and, if you use the iPhone app, your iPhone, so we do not process them.
  • Speech Recognition permission (iPhone app 1.9 or later) — consent, granted and revocable the same way. Transcription runs on your iPhone; we do not receive or process the audio or the text.
  • iCloud sync of text notes — this is your own iCloud storage under your own Apple Account, provided to you by Apple. Apple is the controller for that service; we are not, because we have no access to it and receive nothing from it. Turning off iCloud for VoxWhisper (or signing out) stops the sync and keeps the notes on the watch.
  • If you email us — we process your address and whatever you write, in order to answer you. Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (steps taken at your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in supporting the people who bought the app). Under KVKK, Art. 5(2)(c) and 5(2)(f).
  • Your purchase — the sale is a contract between you and Apple. Apple is the merchant of record and its own controller; we receive no name, address, email or payment detail from it, only anonymous sales totals in App Store Connect.

There is no automated decision-making and no profiling of any kind, in the sense of GDPR Art. 22 or KVKK Art. 11(g).

Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15–22) and KVKK (Article 11) you have the right to ask us for: access to any personal data we hold about you; rectification of anything inaccurate; erasure; restriction of processing; portability of data you gave us, in a machine-readable form; and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where you gave consent — for example a permission prompt — you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting anything done before. KVKK Art. 11 additionally gives you the right to learn whether your data is processed, to be told the purpose, to learn the third parties it is transferred to, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to exercise them: email brkekahraman@icloud.com and say what you want. We reply within one month (GDPR Art. 12(3)); KVKK applications are answered within 30 days under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. There is no charge.

In practice, for most of it you do not need us. The data VoxWhisper works with lives on your watch. Delete it in the app, revoke a permission in Settings, or delete the app — and it is gone. We could not retrieve, restore or hand it over even if you asked us to, because we never had a copy.

Complaining about us

Tell us first if you can — it is usually faster. But you do not have to, and you always have the right to complain to a supervisory authority:

  • Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu / Kurulu — kvkk.gov.tr
  • EU / EEA: the data protection authority of the country where you live, work, or where you think the problem happened — the list is at edpb.europa.eu
  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office — ico.org.uk

How long anything is kept

  • On your watch: for as long as you keep it there. VoxWhisper does not expire or auto-delete your content; deleting the app removes its container and everything in it.
  • Support email: kept only as long as we need it to deal with your message, and deleted within 12 months at the latest — unless a law requires us to keep a specific record longer (for example Turkish tax and commercial bookkeeping rules, if your message relates to a purchase).
  • Everything else: nothing, because there is nothing. We run no database, keep no backup of your content, and write no log file that contains your data.

International transfers

  • We do not transfer your data anywhere, because we do not receive it.
  • Email is the one exception. If you write to us, your message reaches us in Türkiye — outside the EEA and the UK, and Türkiye has no EU adequacy decision. That transfer happens because you chose to contact us and is necessary to answer you (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)). We use it for nothing else and pass it to nobody.
  • The iCloud sync of your text notes is Apple's service on Apple's infrastructure, wherever Apple operates it, under Apple's privacy policy and Apple's own transfer safeguards. We are not a party to it, hold no key and receive no copy.

Children

Bu kopya cihazdan cihaza gider; arada bizim ya da bir başkasının sunucusu yoktur. iPhone'da bir sesli notu silmek yalnızca iPhone'daki kopyayı siler. iPhone uygulaması yoksa sesli notlar, sen silene ya da uygulamayı kaldırana kadar saatte kalır.

Özetler ve yapılacaklar — iPhone’unda (2.0 ve sonrası)
