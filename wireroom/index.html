News from the source, on one wire

Wireroom is an app for iPhone and iPad. It carries the releases that institutions publish themselves and the work of newsrooms that publish under an open licence. Each source sits on the day line where its own local time is right now, and every dispatch can be read in full inside the app: its text is set from the source's own page in a reading view, not rewritten, with the source named and linked.

Dispatches written in another language can be translated on your device (iOS 18 and later). A picture desk shows pictures and films that observatories publish for reuse, one to a screen. Under For you you can follow any of 33 topics; For you can also learn from what you open, on your device only, and forgets it when you switch that off. The wire itself always stays in the order it was filed.

You can add any RSS or Atom feed of your own, podcasts included, set watchwords and quiet words, have the wire or a single dispatch read aloud, and play audio dispatches inside the app.

Support

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, your device and the steps that lead to the problem.

A source shows "no answer this time". The source's server did not return its feed when Wireroom asked. Pull down on the wire to ask again. What was read earlier stays on the wire.

My feed address is not accepted. Wireroom reads RSS and Atom feeds over a secure (https) connection. An address that only answers over plain http, or that returns a web page instead of a feed, cannot be added.

Audio stops when I leave the app. Playing on in the background and on the lock screen is part of the Listen subscription. Without it, audio plays while Wireroom is in front.

Subscriptions. Edition+ and Listen are separate subscriptions; you can hold either or both. Manage or cancel them in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. To bring a purchase to another device, open Your edition, Subscription, Restore purchases.

Pictures and films are shown only from NASA, the European Southern Observatory, ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb and NSF NOIRLab, which publish them for reuse; each is shown with its credit.

Excerpts are shortened by Wireroom; full texts are shown as the source published them. A translation is made by your device, is marked as a translation, and is never shared from the app; sources whose licence allows no derivatives, such as The Conversation, are shown in their own words only. Where a source marks a single picture or text as an exception to its terms, the source's own page, linked from every dispatch, is what counts. No source endorses Wireroom, and Wireroom is not affiliated with any of them. Feeds you add yourself are read by your device from the address you give; their content belongs to their publishers.
