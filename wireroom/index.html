News from the source, on one wire

Wireroom is an app for iPhone and iPad. It carries the releases that institutions publish themselves and the work of newsrooms that publish under an open licence. Each source sits on the day line where its own local time is right now, and every dispatch opens on the source's own page.

You can add any RSS or Atom feed of your own, podcasts included, set watchwords that are marked on the wire, and play audio dispatches inside the app.

Support

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, your device and the steps that lead to the problem.

A source shows "no answer this time". The source's server did not return its feed when Wireroom asked. Pull down on the wire to ask again. What was read earlier stays on the wire.

My feed address is not accepted. Wireroom reads RSS and Atom feeds over a secure (https) connection. An address that only answers over plain http, or that returns a web page instead of a feed, cannot be added.

Audio stops when I leave the app. Playing on in the background and on the lock screen is part of the Listen subscription. Without it, audio plays while Wireroom is in front.

Subscriptions. Edition+ and Listen are separate subscriptions; you can hold either or both. Manage or cancel them in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. To bring a purchase to another device, open Your edition, Subscription, Restore purchases.
