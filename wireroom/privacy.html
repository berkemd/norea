Privacy policy · last updated 11 November 2026

Privacy

Wireroom has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. We, the developer, receive no information about you or about what you read.

What stays on your device

Your edition is stored only in the app's own storage on your device: the desks you switched on, the feeds you added, your watchwords, and a copy of recent dispatches so the wire opens quickly and can be read offline. It is not synced and is deleted when you delete the app.

What leaves your device, and to whom

To show the wire, your device contacts the publishers directly:

  • Feeds. Your device asks each source on your edition, and each feed you added, for its feed. As with any web request, that publisher (and the hosting company it uses) sees your IP address and that the request came from Wireroom.
  • Pictures and audio. A picture shown beside a dispatch, and audio you play, are loaded from the address given in the publisher's feed, which may be a hosting or podcast-delivery service the publisher chose.
  • Reading a dispatch. A dispatch opens on the publisher's own page in the system's in-app browser. From then on that site's own privacy policy and cookies apply, exactly as in Safari.

Wireroom adds nothing to these requests that identifies you: no account, no advertising identifier, no tracking code. Your watchwords are matched on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and tells the app only which subscriptions are active on your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Permissions

Wireroom asks for no device permission: not location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or notifications.

Children

Wireroom is a news reader for a general audience and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact
