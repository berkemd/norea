Koine — Privacy

Koine makes no app network requests. It does not send your answers or reading progress to us, analytics providers or AI services.

What Koine collects from you

We do not receive data from the app. Koine has no account, sign-in, analytics, advertising identifier or AI grading service. Your English word answers are checked by you, not sent elsewhere for grading.

What is stored in the app

Koine stores the following in its local app storage:

  • Your typed word answers, the word and context identifiers, your self-check result and the time of each check.
  • Unique attempt identifiers, used to avoid counting the same saved check twice.
  • The lesson you are reading, your paragraph and step, selected comprehension answers, and completion status and times.
  • Later word-review answers, results and times, together with earlier word-check history.
  • Your setting for whether proper names count as recognisable in the coverage estimate.

These records let the app resume a reading and choose words for later review. Koine does not operate a server sync service or transmit these records through its own network requests.

iOS backups and deleting the app

iOS may include local app data in system backups, depending on your Apple and device backup settings. Those system backups are separate from Koine and are managed by Apple and the operating system.

Deleting the app through iOS removes its local app data. Existing system backups are managed separately through your Apple and device settings. Do not delete the app as a way to retry a save or load error; see the support guide first.

Permissions

Koine does not request camera, microphone, photo-library or location access. Its reading library is included in the app and is available offline.

Content and sources

The starter library includes 6 Spanish readings, 24 words in context and 12 comprehension questions, with English explanations. The separate coverage view uses a frequency list derived from Tatoeba sentence exports, with source and licence attribution displayed in the app. Neither is fetched at runtime.
