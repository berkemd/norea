Koine — Support

Questions: hello@norea.studio

Why is my coverage a range instead of a number?

Because it is one. Your recall of any given word is an estimate from a handful of answers and a model of forgetting, and both carry uncertainty. Reporting a single percentage would be reporting a confidence we never measured.

Why did my coverage go down when I did not forget anything?

Time passed. A word answered correctly once is not the same as a word answered correctly once six weeks ago. Koine recomputes from the dates rather than leaving a word in a "learned" column forever, so coverage falls as recall decays and rises again when you review.

What is the difference between the two parts of the band?

The random part is the noise of estimating from few answers; it narrows every time you answer, and it partly cancels out across thousands of words. The systematic part comes from not knowing your own forgetting curve; it points the same way for every word, so it accumulates, and more questions will not shrink it. Only elapsed time gives us anything to measure. Koine tells you which one is currently larger, because the two ask for different things: more questions, or more time.

Why does it say it "cannot tell yet"?

Because your band crosses the threshold. If the low end of the range is below 95% and the high end is above it, then whether you are over the line is genuinely unknown, and rounding the midpoint would invent a verdict. When the whole band clears the threshold, Koine says so plainly.

Should I count proper names?

That is your call, and the switch is on the main screen. Names are 2.89% of this corpus, and reading one does not require learning it — you recognise it. But we never measured whether you recognise a given name, so counting them is an assumption. Koine will not make it silently on your behalf.

What is the corpus, and why does it matter?

3,209,861 tokens of everyday Spanish sentences from the Tatoeba project. It is not news prose, and that changes the answer: the same vocabulary covers a different share of a newspaper than of everyday speech. A frequency list is not the language — it is one measured sample of one kind of text, and Koine shows which.

Why does 96.1% appear as a ceiling?

Our list holds 46,335 forms, which together are 96.08% of the corpus. Proper names are another 2.89%. The remaining 1.03% is roughly 33,000 forms that occur once — mostly typos and one-off inflections in a volunteer corpus. They are not learnable vocabulary, so they are not in the list, but their share is shown rather than hidden.

When will other languages arrive?

When their corpora are measured with the same care and the licence is clean. Shipping a language whose frequency list we had not measured would be the exact thing this app exists not to do.

Does it work offline?

It only works offline, in the sense that it never goes online. The whole corpus is compiled into the app before it ships.
