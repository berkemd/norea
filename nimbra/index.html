The forecast, drawn as the hour it describes

Nimbra is a weather app for iPhone and iPad. Its picture is not a photograph or a set of icons: it is drawn from the forecast for the hour on screen. The sun sits where it really is in the sky for your place and time, the moon shows its real phase, and the cloud, rain, snow, fog and wind are the forecast's. Drag the time slider and the scene moves through the next two days.

Below the scene Nimbra says the next 24 hours in one plain sentence and tells you what the next 36 hours are good for (a walk, a bike ride, drying laundry, looking at the stars), with the best hours for each or the reason there are none.

Support

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, your device and the steps that lead to the problem.

"The forecast service did not answer." Nimbra could not reach MET Norway. Pull down to try again. The last forecast it has is shown until a newer one arrives.

How fresh is the forecast? MET Norway says how long each forecast is valid, and Nimbra asks again only after that, as the service's terms require. "Updated" at the bottom of the screen shows when.

Widgets. Add a Nimbra widget from the Home Screen or the Lock Screen. It shows the place that is open in the app, in the landscape you chose. Open Nimbra once after installing so the widget knows your place.

Units. Temperature and wind follow your device's region settings.

Subscriptions. Manage or cancel Nimbra+ in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. To bring a purchase to another device, open Nimbra's settings and choose Restore purchases.
