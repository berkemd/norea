Privacy policy · last updated 12 November 2026

Privacy

Nimbra has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. We, the developer, receive no information about you or where you are.

What stays on your device

The place you are looking at, your saved places, the landscape chosen for each, and the latest forecast. The place and landscape on screen are also kept in the device keychain, in a group only Nimbra and its own widgets can read, so the widgets can show the same place. None of this is synced or sent to us.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). To get a forecast, your device (the app or its widget) sends MET Norway the coordinates of the place, rounded to two decimals, about one kilometre. As with any web request, MET Norway sees your IP address and that the request came from Nimbra. See MET Norway's privacy statement.
  • Apple, for place names. When you search for a place, or use your location, the text or coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to find the place and its name and time zone.
  • Apple, for purchases. Subscriptions are bought and managed through the App Store. Nimbra learns only whether Nimbra+ is active.

Nimbra adds nothing to these requests that identifies you: no account, no advertising identifier, no tracking code.

Location

Location is optional: you can search for a place instead. If you allow it, Nimbra asks for your location only while you are using the app, at reduced (kilometre) accuracy, to show the forecast where you are.

Children

Nimbra is a general-audience weather app and is not directed at children.
