Ostinato

Privacy

Ostinato collects nothing, sends nothing and keeps everything it needs on your own devices.

Updated 29 September 2026 (Ostinato 2).

Data collected

None. No analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no account, no email address. The app contains no network code: a build gate fails the release if a network framework appears anywhere in it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Ostinato reads only your sleep (sleep analysis) for the last night, so today's alertness curve starts from how you actually slept. It reads no other type of health data and it never writes anything to Apple Health.

Health data is used only on your device to draw your curve and plan your day. It is never sent anywhere, never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Ostinato. You can use Ostinato without Apple Health and enter last night by hand.

The calendar

If you allow calendar access, Ostinato reads only the start and end times of events, so it can plan around the times you are busy. It does not read titles, guests, notes, locations or URLs, and it does not copy or transmit anything it reads. A build gate enforces this. The calendar is optional.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings: sleep times on work days and free days, workday window, chronotype.
  • Your tasks, the plans Ostinato wrote for you, your alertness check-ins and how your blocks went.
  • A small summary of today's plan shared with Ostinato's own widgets, Apple Watch app and Live Activity, so they can show what is happening now.

All of it stays in Ostinato's storage on your iPhone and, for the watch, on your paired Apple Watch. Settings → Erase everything deletes it in one step; deleting the app deletes it too.

Notifications and Live Activities

Block reminders, the morning note and the Live Activity are created on your iPhone. There is no push server and no push token is requested.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit and verified on the device. Ostinato has no server and receives no purchase data.

Children

Ostinato is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
