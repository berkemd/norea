Ostinato plans your own tasks around your predicted alertness: hard work in your sharpest hours, routine in the dips, breaks after focus — around the meetings you already have.

Getting started

  1. Your body clock. Enter when you sleep on work days and on free days. Ostinato works out your chronotype and social jetlag the way the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire does.
  2. Your workday. The window Ostinato plans inside.
  3. Your three most important tasks. Give each one a kind of work (focus, learning, creative, people, routine) and a rough length.
  4. Optional connections: calendar (busy times only), Apple Health (sleep only), notifications.
  5. Tap Plan my day.

During the day

Today shows your day as a score: each block beside the part of the alertness curve it sits on. Tap a block to see why it is there. When a demanding block ends, one tap says how it went — done, took longer, or didn't happen. The current block also appears as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and on Apple Watch.

How it learns you

Check-ins on the Karolinska Sleepiness Scale ("How alert are you now?") move your curve earlier or later and sharper or sleepier. Finished blocks teach Ostinato how long your kinds of work really take. Both stay on your device.

Frequently asked

Is the curve a measurement?

No. It is a prediction from a published, validated model (the three-process model of alertness). It is not a medical device; don't use it to decide whether it is safe to drive.

What is free, and what does the purchase add?

Free forever: today's plan for your three most important tasks, the alertness curve, check-ins, learning, replanning, the watch app, widgets and the Live Activity. Ostinato Unlimited is a one-time purchase — no subscription — for unlimited tasks, planning tomorrow, export to calendar files and your full history.

How do I restore the purchase?

Settings (gear icon on Today) → Restore purchase.

Does anything leave my device?

No. There is no account, no server and no network code. See Privacy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Erase everything.
