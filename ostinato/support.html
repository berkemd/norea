Ostinato support

Plan what matters, then recover the rest of your day when it changes.

Getting started

  1. Choose Plan today and set the time window you have left.
  2. Add up to three outcomes. Give each a name, remaining minutes and priority. A deadline is optional.
  3. Add fixed busy times manually, or choose optional calendar access to import busy intervals.
  4. Build the plan. Review the timeline and any work that could not fit.

During the day

Mark blocks complete when the work is done. You can undo a completion or edit remaining work. After an interruption, use Replan the rest of today to enter lost time or a new meeting. Completed work remains in the plan.

Free and Unlimited

One plan and one replan each day are free. The existing Ostinato Unlimited one-time purchase adds further replans and text export. No subscription is required. Open Settings to find the purchase and Restore controls. Your stored plan and completed work remain available when the free replan limit is reached.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings, then the Unlimited offer and Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that owns the eligible purchase. If restoration fails, the app shows its result; it does not grant an unverified purchase.

Watch and languages

The companion Watch app can show the plan and send completion actions when the paired devices can communicate. The iPhone and iPad interface is available in English and Turkish.

Local data

Plans and completion history are stored on your device. Optional calendar access reads busy times only. Remove the app from each device to remove its local copy; Apple’s backups and purchase records are separate. See Privacy.
