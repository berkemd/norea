Ostinato

Your hardest work, in your sharpest hours.

Ostinato predicts today's alertness from how you slept, your body clock and your calendar — and plans your own tasks around it.

A real model, not a guess

Sleep pressure, your circadian rhythm and the fog after waking, with the parameters of a published validation study (Ingre et al. 2014), expressed on the Karolinska Sleepiness Scale. Your chronotype comes from your sleep on work days and free days, as in the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire.

Planned, then checked

Demanding work goes where you are sharpest and never where you are predicted to be sleepy. Every focus block is followed by a break; the day opens with a warm-up and closes with a wind-down. An independent checker verifies every plan against these rules.

It learns you

One-tap check-ins teach it when your rhythm really runs; finished blocks teach it how long your work really takes.

Everywhere you look

  • Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Apple Watch: what now, how long, what next.
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, a morning note with your sharpest two hours.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics. Calendar: busy times only. Apple Health: sleep only, read only. Everything stays on your devices.

Free, with one optional purchase

Free forever for your three most important tasks a day. Ostinato Unlimited is a one-time purchase — no subscription.
