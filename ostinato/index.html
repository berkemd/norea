When your day changes, see what still fits.

Plan up to three meaningful outcomes around the time and commitments you actually have.

Make a plan from real constraints

Name each outcome, estimate its remaining minutes, set a priority and optionally add a deadline. Add busy times manually or import calendar busy intervals. See the proposed schedule and the work that could not fit.

Recover after an interruption

Enter lost time or a new meeting. Replan unfinished work without clearing completed work. Mark a block complete, undo a completion or edit remaining work. Your plan and completion history are saved on your device.

On your iPhone, iPad and paired Apple Watch

The companion Watch app can receive the plan and send completion actions. Delivery depends on the paired devices being connected. The app interface is available in English and Turkish.

Private planning

No account is required. Optional calendar access reads busy times, not event titles or notes. Plans stay in the app’s local storage and may be shared with your paired Watch. Read the privacy details.

Free, with an optional one-time purchase

One plan and one replan each day are free. Ostinato Unlimited unlocks further replans and text export with one payment. No subscription is required. Purchase and Restore are in Settings.
