Shop the look, with intent

Closetwise ranks pieces by how many new outfits they would make with what you already own. A plain, well-cut T-shirt is the piece that most often comes out on top: it goes under a blazer for work, with chinos for a casual day and with dark trousers in the evening. If yours are worn out, start there: men's T-shirts at Uniqlo.

Before buying anything new, check three things: it goes with at least three pieces you own, it suits the dress code you need most, and you would wear it in more than one season.
