Closetwise: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Closetwise is built so that your wardrobe never leaves your device.

What Closetwise collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Photos and recognition

When you photograph clothes or choose photos, recognition (what kind of piece it is and its colour) runs on your device with Apple's on-device frameworks. Photos are stored only in the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to add pieces.

What stays on your device

Your pieces, photos, style rules, the outfits you mark as worn and your settings (whose clothes, which country, used for the season and which shops to search) are kept in the app's storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection. You can export them as a file or erase them in Settings at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Shop links

When you tap a shopping idea, Closetwise opens the shop's own search page (for example Amazon, Trendyol or Google Shopping) in your browser with a description such as "navy chinos". Nothing about you or your wardrobe is sent. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Closetwise Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Children

Closetwise is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
