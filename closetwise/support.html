Closetwise: Support

Adding clothes

Wardrobe tab › + › Take a photo or Choose from Photos. Lay several pieces apart on a plain surface and they are recognised separately. You can correct the kind of piece, colour, pattern, fabric and season before saving. To look around first, tap Try a sample wardrobe; remove the sample pieces later from the Wardrobe tab.

Why no outfit today?

An outfit needs a top and a bottom that go together, or a dress, for the current season and the occasion you chose. The Shop tab shows which piece would help most.

Shopping ideas

Ideas are ranked by how many new complete outfits each piece would make with what you own. Tapping one opens a shop's own search. Closetwise does not sell anything; orders, delivery and returns are with the shop.

Restore Pro on a new device

Style tab › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel or change a subscription

Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Moving to a new phone

Your wardrobe lives only on your device. Style tab › Settings › Export my wardrobe saves it as a file; an iCloud or encrypted computer backup of the phone also keeps it.
