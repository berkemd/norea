A brag book that stays private

  • Your self: name a work topic in your own words — a project, a responsibility, a skill — and add examples: what happened, your contribution, a link or a PDF.
  • Preparation: see which claims already have supporting examples before a review or interview.
  • Share only what is needed: pick the examples and the exact fields (topic, contribution, notes, links, dates, purpose), read the full text, then send it with the iOS share sheet.
  • Private by design: no account, no sign-in, no device permissions, no analytics. Your records stay on your device.

Everything is marked as self-reported. Eidos does not score, verify or certify you, and it does not contact employers.

Eidos Pro

The free version covers a full first review. Eidos Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that removes the limits on new topics, examples and PDFs. Reading, editing, deleting and sharing what you saved stay free, and nothing is locked if Pro ends.

What the scores mean

The Showcase and Personas tabs show a local demonstration model. Any score there is a software calculation, not a validated assessment. Do not use these numbers to hire, reject or certify anyone.
