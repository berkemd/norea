Start with one real work example

Before adding another adjective to a CV, write down the situation, your own contribution, the outcome and a source you are allowed to share. Use the same example for an interview, a project introduction or a performance conversation.

  1. Choose one task you need to explain.
  2. Separate your contribution from the team’s result.
  3. Add a source only if you have permission to share it. A link alone does not verify a claim.
  4. Remove confidential details before sharing.

Download the free work-example worksheet (.txt). It works in your usual notes app; no Eidos account, payment or upload is needed.

What is being developed

A private preparation workflow lets you save work notes and source links, see which claims have examples, set the purpose of a summary, and preview the exact fields you choose to share.

This workflow is a tested development candidate, not yet the App Store release or an available public beta. It does not contact employers, certify skills or guarantee an outcome. We will update this page when it is actually available.

Help us test whether this deserves another app

If you recently prepared examples for an interview, client proposal or review, tell us what was difficult with your existing method. Did finding an example, choosing what to disclose, or adapting it to the audience take the most effort?

Try the worksheet with one non-confidential example. Tell us whether it helped and whether you would use it again. There is no reward for praise, no request for a public rating, and no purchase required.

Send optional feedback by email. Nothing is sent until you send it in your mail app. Please omit client, patient, employer-confidential and identifying third-party information. Your message and email address reach the developer; mention if you do not want a reply.

What the scores mean

Any displayed score is a local software calculation, not a validated assessment. Tapping a validation step, granting an internal permission or adding more records does not independently verify a person. Do not use these numbers to hire, reject or certify anyone.

Your data and costs

Version 1.0 has no developer account, connected backend or analytics, and no in-app purchase or subscription. Its internal permission controls are calculation inputs, not access to device sensors. Email feedback is separate from the app. See the privacy policy.
