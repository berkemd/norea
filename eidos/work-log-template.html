Weekly work log template

A free guide from Eidos · the five-minute habit that makes review season easy

By December most people can remember the last six weeks of their year. A work log fixes that. It is the raw material for a brag document, a self-review or an interview answer: a few lines a week, written while the details are still fresh.

The five-minute Friday template

Answer the prompts that apply. One line each is enough.

Finished: what did I complete or move forward this week?

Unblocked: who did I help, and what could they do afterwards?

Learned: what do I know or do now that I did not last week?

Proof: where can this be seen: a document, ticket, message or number?

Topic: which area of my work does each line belong to?

What makes an entry useful later

  • Your part, not only the team's. "We launched" tells a reviewer nothing about you. Add the sentence that starts with "I".
  • A date. Month and year is enough, and the log supplies it for free.
  • A source. A link or file name now saves an hour of searching in December.
  • The unglamorous work. Clean-up, documentation and covering for a colleague are exactly what nobody remembers at review time.

What to leave out

  • Client names, personal data and anything under a confidentiality agreement. Describe the kind of work instead.
  • Company documents you are not allowed to keep on a personal device. Note where they live, not what they contain.
  • Complaints about colleagues. A log is a record of your work.

The monthly roll-up

  1. Read the month's entries and group them by topic.
  2. Under each topic keep the one or two strongest examples and write them out in full: situation, your contribution, outcome, source.
  3. Note the topics that have no example. That is next month's question for your manager, or a sign the topic no longer describes your job.
  4. Delete nothing. Weak entries often become the answer to an unexpected interview question.

When the review form arrives

Match the form's questions to your topics, pick the examples each question needs and rewrite them for the reader. The brag document template covers the structure, and self-review examples shows how a vague sentence becomes a specific one.
