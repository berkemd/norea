Eidos — Support

Updated 24 October 2026. Applies to Eidos 1.1 and later unless stated otherwise.

For a problem or a question, email brkekahraman@icloud.com. Include the app version, device and steps to reproduce. Do not send private records, credentials or screenshots containing other people’s information.

Does a score or “Verified” label prove my skills?

No. Scores and labels in the Showcase and Personas tabs are outputs of a local demonstration model. Advancing a validation step is not a check by an employer, credential issuer or independent reviewer. An internal permission does not prove identity or competence. Do not present a score as a verified credential or use it for decisions about other people.

Why are there no real candidates or employers?

Eidos has no connected employer network or candidate service. Its Employer screen demonstrates matching rules. Changing your score cannot enroll you in a real pool, submit an application or bring you a job.

Are the showcase and personas real users?

The showcase and persona library contain bundled examples, including historical figures, used to illustrate how local profile weights change. Their local calculations and source-weight changes do not demonstrate independent verification, real market activity or a live person’s endorsement. “Memorial only” is an internal representation label, not legal clearance to reuse third-party material.

How do I prepare and share a summary?

Since version 1.1 the app opens on Your self: add a work topic in your own words, then examples with what happened, your contribution, a link or a PDF. Preparation shows which claims already have supporting examples. To share, open Create a shareable summary, pick the examples and the exact fields, read the full text, then send it with the iOS share sheet. Nothing is selected for you.

Can I prepare a summary without installing the app?

Yes. The free browser worksheet lets you draft work examples, select fields, preview the exact text and copy it. This separate web tool does not save documents or verify credentials. Its fields are held in page memory. Copy what you need before refreshing or closing the page; there is no draft recovery service.

If your browser refuses clipboard access, select the preview and copy it manually. Clear worksheet resets the page's fields and preview; it cannot remove anything already copied, emailed or saved elsewhere. You can still download the text template without JavaScript.

Does Eidos use the internet or charge a subscription?

Eidos has no account, backend or analytics and works offline. The app is free to download. Eidos Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that removes the free limits on new work topics, examples and PDFs; the yearly plan can start with a free trial for eligible new subscribers. The price and trial are shown before you confirm. Reading, editing, deleting and sharing what you already saved are always free, and nothing is locked if Pro ends. Restore a purchase from the Eidos Pro screen; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Language

The app follows supported device languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español and 日本語; otherwise it uses English.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device. Offloading removes the app software while retaining its documents and data on the device. Device backups or copies you make outside the app may remain under your backup or recipient’s controls. The developer cannot remotely erase them. For an emailed support message, ask the developer to delete the message.

Tell us about a real task

Send optional feedback about preparing a work example with your existing method. Do not include confidential source material. Email is voluntary; no review, rating or payment is required.
