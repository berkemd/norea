Neuma — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026.

Neuma has no account, no server, and no analytics. There is no sign-in, no email address is collected, and the app contains no telemetry endpoint.

What Neuma stores, and where

Your notes, attachments, voice memos, graph, and settings — in Neuma's container on your device. If you enable sync, a copy is written to your own iCloud private database under your Apple Account. That database is not readable by me; Apple holds it on your behalf.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing that I receive. Neuma contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no third-party purchase SDK, and no code that contacts a Neuma server, because there is no Neuma server. The only network traffic the app can cause is Apple's own — StoreKit for purchases and CloudKit for sync — and, if you configure Mind Bridge, a request to the AI provider you chose, using your own key.

Mind Bridge

Off by default. When you add a provider key it is stored in the iOS Keychain and never leaves the device except as the Authorization header on a request to that provider. What may be included in such a request is governed by a consent matrix you control per cluster and per tag; a kill switch disables the bridge entirely. Your relationship with that provider is yours alone, under their terms — Neuma is not in the middle of it.

What the developer receives

Nothing, unless you email me. Then I have your message and your address.

Purchases

Neuma Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the app reads whether a subscription is active directly from StoreKit on the device. Payment details never reach the app, and Apple's sales reports to me do not identify you.

Privacy manifest

Neuma's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares an empty set of collected data types. That is not an oversight — it is the literal truth for this build, and the repository has a test that fails if an account system, a telemetry endpoint, or a third-party purchase SDK is ever added back without the declaration changing with it.

Children

Neuma is not directed at children and collects nothing identifying about anyone.

Your rights

There is no server-side copy to access, correct, export, or delete. Your export already exists: Settings → Export & Import writes the vault in a documented format. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy; removing Neuma's iCloud data in iOS Settings removes the other.
