A small habit with a useful result

  1. Capture one thought you want to keep.
  2. Find it through local words, phrases and tags.
  3. Ask yourself what useful next step it suggests.
  4. With version 1.3, share an edited excerpt and a Neuma link when you choose.

Start free

Local notes and search are included. Version 1.3 also includes Recall discovery and excerpt sharing. If you turn on iCloud sync, the free plan mirrors your 250 most recent items. Neuma Plus removes that sync limit. Older local items are not deleted because of the limit.

Monthly and annual subscriptions show their current prices in the app. Apple handles purchases, renewal, restoration and cancellation.

Private by default, connected by choice

Neuma requires no account for local notes and search. Optional iCloud sync uses your Apple Account. Other available destinations are configured by you. Content sent to a selected destination is governed by that service's terms.

Mind Bridge is off by default. External AI requires your own provider key, permission rules and confirmation of the text to send. A provider may retain that text under its policies. You do not need an external AI account to capture, search or rediscover notes. Read the privacy policy.
