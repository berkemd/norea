From a saved idea to something you can use

For example, bring your notes for an article, a course or a personal project together:

  1. Save the starting point. Write a thought, paste a source link or add a photo.
  2. Make the connection. Put related notes in a topic and link the ideas you want to explore.
  3. Return with a purpose. Search your words and tags, revisit a note through Recall, or share a selected mind map or edited excerpt.

You choose the notes to share. Review the output first; the source notes stay in your vault.

Included free

Local capture, search, Recall and excerpt sharing. If you enable iCloud sync, the free plan mirrors your 250 most recent items. Older local items are not deleted because of that sync limit.

Neuma Plus

Turn on automatic connections and topic placement for new notes, with note analysis enabled. Matching uses text and tags on your device; inspect the reason and undo an automatic change. Plus also removes the 250-item sync limit.

Monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions. See your local price and any eligibility in the app before purchasing. No purchase is needed to start a local collection.

Do I need an AI account or a paid model?

No. Local capture, search, Recall and Plus text-and-tag matching need no external AI account. Optional Mind Bridge is a separate connection you configure yourself.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Open Settings → Neuma Plus → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that purchased it. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Cancel before the renewal date shown by Apple.

Private by default, connected by choice

Neuma requires no account for local notes and search. Optional iCloud sync uses your Apple Account. Other available destinations are configured by you. Content sent to a selected destination is governed by that service's terms.

Mind Bridge is off by default. External AI requires your own provider key, permission rules and confirmation of the text to send. A provider may retain that text under its policies. You do not need an external AI account to capture, search or rediscover notes. Read the privacy policy.
