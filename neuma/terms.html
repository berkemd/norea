Neuma Plus

Neuma Plus is an auto-renewing subscription offered monthly and yearly. The price shown in the app is the App Store's own price for your region. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

The free tier

The free tier is complete and does not expire. Capture, vault, search, graph, synthesis, Mind Bridge, and export are all available without paying. The free tier mirrors the 250 most recent items between your devices; Plus lifts that cap. Items beyond it are never deleted.
